Telol

Designed to be easily controllable, Telols have multiple weaknesses. They were designed to be pests - adaptive, hardy, resistant to dangers of nature, breeding fast and living long. They thrive in cold, hostile environments and outcompete other colonizers by attrition.

Requires Biotech DLC
  • Weak against fire (FireWeakness, FireTerror)
  • Strong against nature (Superclotting, Immunity - Strong)
  • Adaptive and carefull (Fast learning, Slow wound healing)
  • Aggressive but peaceful internally
  • Chemical dependency: Smokeleaf
  • Extended lifespan (Ageless gene)
  • High libido, Floppy ears, Pig nose, Smooth tail
