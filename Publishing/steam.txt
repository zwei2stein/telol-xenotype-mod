[h1]Telol Xenotype Mod[/h1]

A tribal-focused custom xenotype.

Suggested to be run with "High Life" and "Preferred Xenotype" ideologion precept.

[h2]Features[/h2]

[list]
    [*]Telol xenotype: weak against fire, strong against nature.
    [*]Custom solo start with Naked Brutality+ (starts with no research completed, not even tribal ones)
    [*]New faction featuring the Telol race, with selected factions having a chance to include Telol members
    [*]Custom naming schemes for Telol factions and pawns.
[/list]

[h3]Genes[/h3]

[list]
    [*]Nocturnal gene: the Telol hunt at night and sleep during the day.
    [*]Cognitive injury response gene: the Telol are designed to adapt to survival situations.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Why[/h2]

I was annoyed that custom xenotype you make for starting pawns is not integrated into the game at all - the ones you start with are all you get. No recruitable prisoners, no factions - nothing.

So I made this mod to fix that. Feel free to clone it for your own xenotype.
