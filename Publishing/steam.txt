[h1]Telol xenotype mod[/h1]

A tribal start focused custom xenotype.

Suggested to be ran with "High Life" and "Prefered xenotype" ideologion precept .

[h2]Features[/h2] 

[list]
    [*]Telol xenotype, weak against fire, strong against nature.
    [*]Custom solo start with nated brutality+ (starts no research completed at all, even trial ones)
    [*]New faction featuring Telol race and selected factions having chance to have Telol members
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Why[/h2] 

I was annoyed that custom xenotype you make for starting pawns is not being integrated to game at all - the ones you start with are all that you have. No recruitable prisoners, no factions, nothing.

So I made this mod to do so. Feel free to clone it for your own xenotype.
